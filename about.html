I’m a Product Maker with a background in storytelling

My story starts with words. I began my career as a project manager and copywriter, coordinating creative projects for different brands and creating educational content. Then came UX writing, then product design.

Over the past five years I’ve designed B2B and B2C products in startups: first in PropTech, building the foundations of an early-stage product, and then in EdTech, with learning experiences and educational apps during a period of fast growth.

Today I bring both together. I find the problems that matter most to users and the business, prioritise what to build and build it with AI. My years as a copywriter gave me a tool I use every day: telling a strategy so that every team understands why we do what we do, and designing products that users understand and want to use.

I still tell stories, but now I make sure they end in numbers

At Ucademy, I noticed in usage metrics and error reports that students weren’t practising. With the CTO, I defined a new practice experience: six modes, progress by topic and feedback on every answer. It became a key sales argument, and the following quarter, sales doubled.

Read the case study ↗

For a new line of business, an AI study app, I led discovery and experience design. Most students finished onboarding and didn’t start a practice, so we made the first practice the last step of onboarding. Activation over downloads rose from about 7% to about 36%.
