From writing stories to shaping products

My story starts with words. I began my career coordinating creative projects and working in copywriting, writing for different brands and creating educational content.

Little by little, the words led me to the whole experience. First came UX writing, then product design. Over the past five years I’ve designed B2B and B2C products in startups: first in PropTech, with tools for property management, and then in EdTech, with learning experiences and educational apps.

Today I work on strategy, asking the big questions that set a product’s direction, and I build the solutions that answer them.
