Approach

I shape the whole body of a product

A product is one body of many parts: the people who use it, the business that sustains it, the data that explains it, the design that makes it clear and the technology that makes it real. When one part is missing, users feel it.

That’s why I don’t work one discipline at a time. I listen before defining the problem, see the whole picture before deciding, speak for users when they’re not in the room, care about how it looks and feels, and build it with AI, so ideas reach real people fast.

I make products with all five senses

  1. I listen

    Understanding people before defining problems.

    Listening to users, teams and stakeholders to uncover needs, challenge assumptions and consider different perspectives.

  2. I see

    Seeing the bigger picture.

    Connecting user insights, business goals and data to identify opportunities and decide what matters most.

  3. I speak

    The user’s voice, even when they’re not in the room.

    Turning research into clear stories that guide decisions, communicate priorities and keep teams aligned around real user needs.

  4. I taste

    Good design takes good taste.

    Applying visual craft, creativity and aesthetic judgement to create intuitive, cohesive and distinctive product experiences.

  5. I make

    Beyond ideas and prototypes.

    Designing, prototyping and building with AI tools to turn concepts into working products, ready to launch, test and improve.
