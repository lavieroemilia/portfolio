Background

Writing came first. I learned to find the structure underneath the mess and turn complex ideas into something clear and meaningful. That way of thinking has stayed with me ever since, whether I’m working on a product, service, process or experience.

How I work

I start by understanding the problem: talking to people, looking for patterns and identifying friction. I bring different perspectives together, create a shared direction and move things forward through prioritisation, testing and iteration.

The kind of problems I like working on

  1. 01

    People are struggling with an experience, but we don’t fully understand why.

  2. 02

    A service works, but has accumulated friction, complexity or workarounds.

  3. 03

    Different teams see the problem differently and need a shared direction.

  4. 04

    There are plenty of ideas, but no clarity on what matters most.
