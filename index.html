Background

Writing came first. I had a natural ability to find the structure underneath the mess and turn complex ideas into something simple, clear, and atractive. Eventually, I realised that's what a good product does too. A well-designed flow works like a well-told story. It keeps you from walking away before you get to the good part.

How I design

Before I touch the interface, I make sure everyone is looking at the same problem. That alignment is what makes the outcome possible. I use AI to accelerate iteration and leave more space for the work that actually requires judgment. When something ships, I measure whether it moved anything, and if it didn't, that's where the next discovery starts.
