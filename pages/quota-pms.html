Quota

Rental management made simpler for agencies

Timeline
2021 – 2024
Role
Sole Product Designer
Platform
Web app and mobile web
Goal
Trial conversion

The challenge

Rental agencies in Spain had to offer more and more management services, but they still ran them on old processes: spreadsheets, phone calls and disconnected tools. Quota wanted to bring all that work into one platform, simple enough for managers with no time to learn a new system.

What I did

For three years I ran continuous discovery with managers, Customer Care and Sales. I designed the whole platform and built its design system from zero, evolving it with every new module.

The outcome

We launched 15+ features and a gamified insurance app. Managers selected tenants twice as fast and did more of their daily work inside Quota.

Decisions

  1. 01 · Properties and contactsTrial conversion

    Bring the whole portfolio into one place

    Need

    Managers kept their properties, tenants, owners and suppliers across spreadsheets, phones and different tools.

    Decision

    A property list with photos, status and rent at a glance, and one contact book for tenants, owners and suppliers, linked to each rental.

    Result

    Managers no longer had to jump between platforms to manage their properties.

  2. 02 · AccountingTrial conversion

    Make month-end accounting fast

    Need

    Rent, contracts and insurance lived in different places, so closing the month meant cross-checking them by hand.

    Decision

    Accounting inside each rental: tenant and owner balances, every transaction with its status, recurring transactions and invoices. The rent, the contract and the insurance were already in Quota, so accounting built on them.

    Result

    80% said they closed the month faster. Fast accounting in one place also helped bring in new clients.

  3. 03 · Tenant onboardingTrial conversion

    Let candidates fill in their own data

    Need

    Managers collected candidates’ personal and financial data by phone or email, then typed it into their own systems.

    Decision

    An invitation by WhatsApp or email: candidates complete and verify their own data, and the profile arrives filled in, ready for scoring.

    Result

    Managers stopped re-entering data and moved faster to screening.

  4. 04 · Tenant scoringTrial conversion

    Show the reasons behind every score

    Need

    With many candidates and no way to compare them, selection relied on gut feeling.

    Decision

    A score that ranks candidates by credit history, employment and vulnerability risk, with every parameter visible so managers can explain it to owners.

    Result

    2× faster tenant selection, and owners could see the data behind each recommendation.

  5. 05 · IncidentsTrial conversion

    Keep everyone posted on repairs

    Need

    Repairs were coordinated by phone and messages, with tenants, owners and suppliers in separate conversations.

    Decision

    Managers log each incident, assign who is responsible and follow its due date. Tenants and owners are notified automatically.

    Result

    Contact with tenants was centralised in Quota.

  6. 06 · ContractsTrial conversion

    Draft contracts from data already in Quota

    Need

    Managers drafted contracts by hand or paid for e-signature tools that weren’t connected to their data.

    Decision

    A step-by-step flow that fills in the contract with the property, the parties and the rent already in Quota, and sends it for digital signature.

    Result

    40% adopted it in the first weeks after launch.

  7. 07 · Insurance appOperational load

    Turn insurance into self-service

    Need

    Arranging rental insurance meant contacting Quota’s team: a bottleneck for managers and constant work for Quota.

    Decision

    A mobile web app to score tenants and buy insurance on their own. I added progress bars and small rewards to reduce drop-off in a long form, and reused the data already in Quota.

    Result

    50% fewer insurance requests to Customer Care.

Impact

Each module brought one more part of the managers’ work into Quota, and average session time grew 25%. The platform and the insurance app shared one design system, which grew with the product.

Learnings

01

Customer Care is a research team

Customer Care and Sales tickets showed real pain faster and more often than any research session I ran.

02

Less friction doesn’t solve adoption

Agencies that used Quota stayed and used it more. The harder problem was convincing them to switch tools in the first place, and without enough new customers, Quota closed in 2024.

03

Fit the customers who already said yes

Getting an agency to start a trial was the hardest part, so when one did, we focused on a solution that fit them. Before product-market fit, adapting to the customers who already show interest matters more than building features for customers you don’t have yet.
