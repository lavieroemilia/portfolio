Quota

Simplifying a complex rental-management service for agencies and tenants

Quota was an early-stage B2B SaaS startup with an ambitious goal: to build the most complete property management system on the market for rental agencies. As Lead Product Designer, I owned the full design of the platform for three years, from the first research sessions to production.

Team
JR AT CF KM
Role

Lead Product Designer

Service

Research · UX · Design System · Prototyping · User Testing

Date

2021 – 2024

Challenge

New regulations were reshaping the Spanish rental market, forcing real estate agencies to expand their services. Most were doing it without the right tools, managing their entire workflow across spreadsheets, phone calls, and disconnected third-party software.

The design challenge was complexity without overwhelm.

Each module had to be powerful enough to replace a dedicated tool, while staying intuitive for managers with no time to learn a new system from scratch.

Quota offered a one-month free trial, so driving engagement during that window was critical to converting users into paying subscribers.

Continuous discovery

Over three years, I maintained a continuous research practice across three channels:

  • I ran recurring interviews with property managers at different agency sizes and digital maturity levels, focusing on workflow and friction.
  • I kept an ongoing benchmark of direct and indirect competitors, tracking how the market was evolving and where Quota had room to differentiate.
  • And I treated Customer Care and Sales as a permanent insight channel.

What I built and why

Property managers were running their entire workflow outside the platform. Each module was designed to pull one more piece of that workflow in, increasing the value of staying and the cost of leaving during the trial period.

The business goal for all PMS modules was to increase engagement during the trial period to drive subscription conversion.

Below you’ll find a selection of the features I designed across those three years.

Tenant onboarding

Problem

Managers were spending significant time collecting personal and financial data from candidates by phone or email, then re-entering it manually into their own systems.

Solution

An invitation flow via WhatsApp or email where candidates completed and verified their own data. The profile arrived pre-filled on the manager’s side and fed directly into the scoring module.

Result

Managers eliminated manual data entry and moved faster into the qualification step.

Read the case study Streamlining the tenant screening process.

CRM with tenant scoring

Problem

With high candidate volume and no structured comparison tool, selection decisions relied on gut feeling and recency bias.

Solution

A scoring system ranking candidates by credit history, employment status, and vulnerability risk, with each parameter visible so managers understood the reasoning behind every score.

Result

Faster tenant selection and a trust signal with property owners, who could see objective data behind the recommendation.

Contract generation

Problem

Managers drafted contracts manually or paid for external e-signature tools that weren’t connected to any of their property or tenant data.

Solution

A guided step-by-step flow that pulled existing platform data, auto-populated the contract, and sent it for digital signature without leaving the tool.

Result

40% of users adopted it within the first weeks after launch.

Gamified insurance app

Goal

Reduce internal operational load by converting a manual process into self-service.

Problem

Arranging rental insurance required contacting Quota’s team directly, creating a bottleneck for managers and a constant support load for Quota’s staff.

Solution

A standalone web app for quick scoring and insurance purchase. I proposed adding progress bars and small rewards to reduce drop-off in a form-heavy flow, reusing data already in the platform so managers didn’t re-enter anything.

Result

Support requests related to insurance dropped by 50%.

The decision

By mid 2024, Quota couldn’t close another funding round. Retention was good, engagement was up, but the acquisition model hadn’t scaled. Without enough new customers coming in, the revenue base stayed too small to attract investors. The company shut down.

It was the clearest lesson of those three years: a product people use and like can still run out of time if the go-to-market problem isn’t solved.

What I learned

Your Customer Care team is a research team

CC and Sales tickets surfaced real pain faster and more consistently than any research session I ran.

Reducing friction is not the same as solving adoption

The friction that stopped growth wasn’t in the product. It was in convincing agencies to switch in the first place.
