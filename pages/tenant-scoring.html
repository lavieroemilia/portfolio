Quota

Reducing friction in a complex tenant screening process

Quota is a Spanish startup that uses AI to score rental candidates and match them to the right properties. They needed a solution to collect tenant data and documentation faster, so agents could stop spending time on candidates who were never going to qualify.

Team
LC CF KM
Role

Lead Product Designer

Service

Product Strategy · Research & Insights · Interaction · UX Writing · Visual Design · Prototyping

Date

2023

Problem

Agents were losing hours reviewing documents and scheduling visits with candidates who didn’t fit the profile.

Solution

We designed a digital pre-scoring flow that lets agents send a link to prospective tenants and arrange visits only with qualified candidates, saving time on both sides before anyone leaves the house.

The pre-scoring benefits both agents and tenants, since both can assess whether the rental is a good fit before the visit takes place.

Problem

Manual document checks were slow and inconsistent.

Solution

We integrated Open Banking and KYC tools to automatically verify identity and financial information using data from public institutions and banks, through a flow designed to be short, self-explanatory, and easy to complete.

Tenants can verify their income and financial situation through a short, self-explanatory flow, without needing help from the agent.

Problem

Real estate agencies were reluctant to send leads through a third-party platform.

Solution

We gave agencies the ability to customize the onboarding with their own branding, removing that barrier to adoption.

Problem

The flow stalled when tenants didn’t have their flatmates’ information on hand.

Solution

We added an invitation feature so tenants could bring co-applicants into the process directly, giving agencies a complete picture of the group without having to chase everyone individually.

Putting the process in the tenants’ hands gives agencies a complete picture of the group without having to chase every candidate individually.

Problem

Sharing financial data is a significant trust barrier for many users.

Solution

UX Writing was central to solving this: every step included benefit-focused copy, tooltips, and contextual help to reduce anxiety and build confidence throughout the flow.

Impact
