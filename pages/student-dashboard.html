Ucademy

Redesigning the student dashboard

Timeline
Q1 2025
Role
Sole Product Designer
Platform
Web and app
Goal
Activation and engagement

The challenge

Around 5,000 Ucademy students a day landed on a dashboard that didn’t show what to study next or where their courses were. New students got lost, and many turned to Customer Care just to find their way.

What I did

I redesigned the dashboard around today’s study plan, put every course, test and contact one click away, and added an onboarding to get new students started in their first week.

The outcome

Students reach a main action twice as fast, 50%+ follow their study plan and 40%+ of new students complete First steps in their first week.

Decisions

  1. 01 · Welcome tourActivation

    Show new students around

    Need

    Session recordings showed new students clicking everywhere and taking long to reach the main actions.

    Decision

    An optional tour on the first visit: under a minute, seven steps that show where everything is.

    Result

    Students understood the platform from their first visit.

  2. 02 · First stepsActivation

    Give new students somewhere to start

    Need

    New students didn’t know where to begin. To activate them, they had to try the platform’s key features early and feel that getting started was easy.

    Decision

    Four key actions worth points for new students: complete their profile, join a live class, do their first task and take a test.

    Result

    40%+ of new students completed First steps in their first week.

  3. 03 · Study plan and progressEngagement and retention

    Tell students what to study today

    Need

    Exams are stressful, and students didn’t know how to organise their study to be ready on time. Nothing told them what to do each day.

    Decision

    The study plan is the key feature, so it comes first: the first thing students see is what to do today, without having to think. Below, their progress next to the ideal plan shows they’ll reach the exam on time if they follow it.

    Result

    Most active students followed their plan: less to decide every day, and the reassurance of being on track for the exam.

  4. 04 · My coursesLower support costs

    Keep every course one click away

    Need

    Only one course was visible, and switching meant finding a hidden dropdown.

    Decision

    The last three courses they studied, each with its progress and next lesson, plus a page with all of them.

    Result

    Students went straight into their courses, switched between them on their own and stopped asking Customer Care where to find them.

  5. 05 · TestsEngagement and retention

    Bring practice to the front

    Need

    Very few students found Practice, and most didn’t know they could create their own tests.

    Decision

    Tests moved onto the dashboard: their latest results and a button to create their own, so practising is one click away from the study plan.

    Result

    20% more tests created by students.

  6. 06 · RankingEngagement and retention

    Make studying a friendly competition

    Need

    Preparing for an exam is a long race. A touch of gamification keeps it motivating: students didn’t want to fall behind in the ranking.

    Decision

    The monthly ranking sits on the dashboard: their position, the students around them and the points they need to move up. The study streak multiplies those points.

    Result

    Together with the daily tasks, the ranking and the streak brought students back more often.

  7. 07 · Advisor contactLower support costs

    Put help where students look

    Need

    Students didn’t know who to ask, and often wrote to the sales team.

    Decision

    Their advisor’s phone, email and WhatsApp, always on the dashboard. Customer Care asked for it.

    Result

    Messages moved to the right channels.

Impact

Students switched courses without asking Customer Care, and messages reached the right channel. The welcome tour and First steps helped them understand the platform from their first visit.

Learnings

01

Customer Care knows first

What students ask Customer Care shows where the product is failing them. I review those questions alongside the analytics.

02

Decide what onboarding should move

Before building an onboarding, decide which behaviour it should change. Then measure that from day one.

03

Retention lives in the core product

Students completed the tour and First steps, but cancellations depended on the course content. Onboarding opens the door, and what keeps students is what they came for.
